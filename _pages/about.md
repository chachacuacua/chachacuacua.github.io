I am a **Lab Manager and Research Assistant at the SEED Lab, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen**. My research interests span **early social development, autism research, family processes, and technology-mediated assessment and intervention**.

I am interested in how children develop social attention and communication, how parent–child and coparenting relationships shape development, and how developmental assessment can better inform useful and accessible support. I am also interested in technology-mediated approaches including **child–AI interaction, child–robot interaction, serious games, digital health, and telehealth**.

My current research work includes studies with infants, toddlers, and preschool-aged children using **Tobii Pro eye-tracking, behavioral observation, developmental assessment, EEG, and fNIRS**. I also develop interactive research prototypes in **Godot Engine 4**.

## Research interests

- **Early social development:** social attention · social communication · parent–child interaction · social motivation
- **Autism research:** early identification and intervention · developmental assessment · family-centered support
- **Family processes:** coparenting · parent–child relationships · transition to parenthood · family systems
- **Technology-mediated assessment & intervention:** child–AI interaction · child–robot interaction · serious games · digital health · telehealth
