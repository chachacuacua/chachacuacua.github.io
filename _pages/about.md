I am a **Lab Manager and Research Assistant at the SEED Lab, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen**, where I work on early social development, parent–child interaction, and visual attention in infancy and early childhood.

My broader research interest is in making developmental assessment more **actionable**: understanding individual differences well enough that assessment can inform what kind of support may be useful for whom. I am particularly interested in autism research, early identification and intervention, family-centered support, and the use of interactive technologies to connect assessment with accessible intervention.

My current work combines **eye-tracking, behavioral observation, developmental assessment, and quantitative modeling**. I am also developing technology-mediated approaches including serious games and interactive systems, with an interest in how these tools can become engaging, scalable, and usable beyond the laboratory.

## Research interests

- **Early social development:** social attention, social communication, parent–child interaction, social motivation
- **Autism research:** early identification and intervention, developmental assessment, individualized and family-centered support
- **Family processes:** coparenting, parent–child relationships, transition to parenthood, family systems
- **Technology-mediated assessment and intervention:** serious games, child–AI interaction, child–robot interaction, digital health, and telehealth

## Current role

At the SEED Lab, I coordinate concurrent developmental research projects involving families with infants and young children, while contributing to study design, data collection, eye-tracking analysis, observational coding, research outputs, and day-to-day lab operations.

My long-term goal is to work at the intersection of **developmental science, assessment, intervention, and accessible technology**—building tools that help researchers and practitioners understand individual needs and translate that understanding into more useful support.
